A LITTLE ABOUT ME

More than just a name.

I'm exploring the world of technology, learning new skills, and discovering what I can create. This website is a little space to share who I am and where my journey takes me. But wait! Before you proceed...

Here's a little backstory...

I am a 22 year old Zulu girl who grew up at Inanda, in KwaZulu Natal. A high crime, low-class disadvantaged community. I really dislike it when people say I’m from that place, my family is from Eshowe, in KZN and I’d rather be from a forest than Inanda, I'm sorry to say this but I feel strongly about it. I went to kindergarten (Crèche) kwaDlomo Crèche. It was technically a daycare in a small area called Namibia in Inanda, another place my heart breaks for because it has turned into a place I do not recognise, we no longer stay there, if anyone is even safe to do so. One can only hold on to the childhood memories and nolstagia.

I then went to primary school at Khanyanjalo JP School and my first uniform was sewn by my late Grandmother, Nomusa K Sibiya, may her beautiful soul rest in peace. I was a prodigy of the school till the very last class, Grade 4, where my farewell dress was hand sewn by my mother Nelisiwe Sibiya… notice how I’m from a family of ladies with super powers?

Everyone asked me where the dress was from and with a bold chest, chin up, my curly hair that my mom (who was not a hairdresser) curled for me the day of the farewell, and my necklace that was actually waistbeads made by my mom’s late grandmother uMaNgcobo, I proudly told them that “Ngiyenzelwe uMah, izolo. Ayikho ezitolo” (My mom made it for me yesterday. It’s not available in stores.) I wish you were there to see my face!! By that time I had a new born baby brother, no grandmother, and no one else but my mom, and a new view of life because it had gotten TOUGH I then proceeded to Inanda day senior primary school, had God Parents, Nonkosi Nkosi and Senzo Xulu, they played a hug role in my life, I can't even begin to explain, I am forever grateful for them. After passing the seventh grade I went to high school, Inanda Newtown Comprehensive, then the University of the Western Cape, where I am currently in my final year figuring life out.

This is not a bibliography I just want everyone to get it when I say, never once in my life did I think I would be able to create a website or even be where I am today. It’s not even a realistic dream where I’m from. Not to mention that I major in Psychology and English, Tech was really not in my plans, until it was…

I love cooking. The last time I pestered someone about my love for cooking, I proceeded to make them burnt steak when it was time for me to cook for them. I wish I wasn’t the one who insisted on cooking for them, I think about this everyday. I hope the mash and mushroom sauce were at-least edible.

I aim to own a farm in the future. You know, homestead, be off the grid and raise l beautiful happy kids with my husband and cook food I’ve grown, and raised for by passers or anyone who’d care to visit my farm. I already have a husband, my husband just doesn’t know he is my husband yet, I shall update you about that in a few years to come. I’m only 22 after all, If you find this absurd you can imagine how I feel about myself.

My biggest goal is to ensure a comfortable life for my siblings. I didn’t grow up in a nice way, I’m still not. I appreciate everyone who has helped me grow, I will never forget them. The world is not always a kind place. I wish for my siblings to be better than me, and that if and when I bring kids to this world I want to be very sure that they will live a comfortable life while I’m here and when I’m gone. So help me God…

Right that’s all the personal information you’ll get. Hold on to the husband part, check back on the website a few years later and if you’re lucky enough to bump into me while I’m in my oversharing spirit, you only have to ask and I’ll tell you if I’m winning or not. Stay safe. Byee

I know you’re not here for my sob life stories though, off you go now, scroll some more for the actual stuff you’re here for 😅😅😅
